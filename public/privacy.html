Privacy Policy

Timebox YouTube Blocker is built to block YouTube locally in Chrome and keep the payment/download flow simple.

Extension data

The Chrome extension stores its break timer, daily break count, and local settings in Chrome storage on the user’s device. The extension does not sell personal data and does not collect browsing history for advertising.

Website data

This website collects the email address used at checkout so it can create a license key and send recovery emails. Payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by this website.

License recovery

If a customer submits their email on the recovery page and that email is on the paid customer list, the site sends the license key to that email address. If it is not on the list, the site says so.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information on the support page.
