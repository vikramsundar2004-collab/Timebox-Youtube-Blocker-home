Privacy Policy

Timebox YouTube Blocker is built to block YouTube locally in Chrome and keep the download flow simple.

Extension data

The Chrome extension stores its break timer, daily break count, and local settings in Chrome storage on the user’s device. The extension does not sell personal data and does not collect browsing history for advertising.

Website data

This download website is static. It does not collect names, emails, payment details, or license keys. Render or the browser may create ordinary server logs for security and reliability.

Payments

Payments are handled in person. This website does not process card payments.

For privacy questions, use the contact information on the support page.
